Virtual Institute Fellowship

For faculty members and independent researchers. Fellows:

  • Conduct collaborative research projects supported by the institute
  • Present research talks, lectures, or community education webinars
  • Provide specialized consultation for ongoing institute projects
  • Participate in evaluation activities and contribute to national conference proposals
3-year appointment · up to 10 members

Affiliated Faculty Program

Faculty from the College of Education and beyond can be invited to:

  • Lead or collaborate on institute-supported research projects
  • Contribute to collaborative book projects and specialized training initiatives
  • Serve on the TIIER Advisory Council and provide expert community service
  • Engage in grant development and long-term strategic research planning
